The AstrBot Android / Android DIY Sandbox / Code LFA approach was studied to design the
local Ubuntu + Linux QQ + NapCat flow. Cupbox does not embed AstrBot's framework or UI.
Ubuntu base images are bundled at build time. Their component licenses and source
locations are documented in UBUNTU-BASE.txt and retained inside the Ubuntu images.
QQ, NapCat and the Linux launcher are downloaded from their upstreams on demand.
Their respective upstream licenses and terms apply; these components are not bundled in this APK.
